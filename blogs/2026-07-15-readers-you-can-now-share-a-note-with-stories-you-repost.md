The most-requested addition to reposts is now live, plus a few other improvements Images created by Jason Combs // the Medium Brand team. (Excellent) story by Marco Capriz in The Quantastic Journal You can now add a note to any story you repost on Medium. Reposting with a note lets you add up to 280 characters of your own context — why you recommend it, what your reaction was, your favorite line, why this story matters right now, who you think would benefit most from reading it — before sharing it to your network.

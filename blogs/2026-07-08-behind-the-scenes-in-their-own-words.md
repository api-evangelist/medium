How building in the open builds community. (Issue #436) Australian cartoonist James S. Baker has been working in animation since the early 1980s — first with Hanna-Barbera’s Australian studio , where he worked on cartoons based on Popeye and Mork & Mindy , before moving to the United States and eventually ending up on the team designing the visuals for some classic Pixar films, like Finding Nemo and WALL-E .

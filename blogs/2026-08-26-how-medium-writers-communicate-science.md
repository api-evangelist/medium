(Issue #443) Base Image: Katherine Dexter McCormick and other students at MIT circa 1890s, via MIT Museum/Wikimedia Commons Science is one of the most direct ways we deepen our understanding of the world, but it can sometimes feel opaque to the non-scientists among us. There’s a thriving community of writers, publications, labs, and researchers on Medium taking science out of the peer-reviewed papers and hyped headlines and into the lives of real readers.
